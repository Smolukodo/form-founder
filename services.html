Common Questions

Answers for founders.

What is the typical timeline for a branding or web project?

A focused brand identity or editorial web project typically spans 3 to 6 weeks. Complete multi-discipline engagements (Branding + Custom Web Design) generally run 6 to 8 weeks. We work in disciplined sprints to maintain velocity.

How do you work with founders located in different time zones?

Our studio is rooted in Nigeria with a seamless remote workflow. We collaborate with founders across the UK, Europe, North America, and Africa using asynchronous updates, Loom video walkthroughs, and scheduled milestone alignment calls.

Do I own all the final files and intellectual property?

Yes, 100%. Upon final project completion and handover, you retain full ownership of all vector files, source code, typography licenses advice, publication files, and master assets.

What if I need ongoing design and digital content support?

Following an initial branding or web project, we offer selective studio advisory retainers for founders who want ongoing editorial content and design direction.

Ready to begin your project?

Tell us about your brand goals, web requirements, or publishing vision. We'll tailor a clear proposal.
